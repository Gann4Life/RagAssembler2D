# 5.5 Tools
The tools live in a **floating toolbar overlay inside the Viewport panel**, not in a dock panel. It is always visible, has no toggle and is not listed in the Window menu.

**Layout.**
- A sibling of the viewport host inside the Viewport panel, before it in document order, stacked over it in the same grid cell, top-left with an 8 px margin. The Viewport panel isolates its stacking context, so the overlay never covers other panels or popups, and the overlay never changes the size of the host.
- A vertical strip: panel surface at 92 % opacity, 6 px radius, and 4 px from its outer edge to the buttons (1 px border plus 3 px padding). Items are 32 × 32 px buttons with 20 px icons and separators 5 px tall (a 1 px line, as wide as a button, centered), with 2 px gaps between consecutive items. One column is 40 × 265 px.
- Buttons in four groups with separators: selection (Select, Select Box), manipulation (Pose), creation (Circle, Box, Polygon), joints (Hinge Joint). Moving and rotating belong to Select; there is no separate Manipulate tool.
- **Two columns** when the strip and its two 8 px margins do not fit the viewport host's height, i.e. when the host is shorter than 265 + 16 = 281 px: the first column holds selection and manipulation, the second creation and joints, each with a separator between its two groups, 4 px apart; 76 × 149 px, set by the taller second column. The separator between manipulation and creation is not shown. Button order (document, focus and keyboard) does not change. The Viewport minimum height of 168 px ([§5.2](05-2-panels.md#52-panels)) fits this layout (149 + 16 = 165 px).
- The overlay's current width (40 or 76 px) is the "Tools overlay width" that framing insets by ([§5.4](05-4-viewport.md#54-viewport)).
- Pointer presses, moves and the wheel over the overlay never reach the viewport: no tool input, no hover, no navigation; a wheel over it neither zooms nor scrolls the page.
- Loading by drag & drop listens on the whole Viewport panel, so a drop on the overlay also loads ([§5.3](05-3-header.md#53-header)); the overlay never stops drag events.

**Icons.** Inline SVG drawn in the repository, no third-party assets and no external references: `viewBox` 24, no fill, stroke `currentColor` at 1.5, round caps and joins; small solid details (dots, the pin) may fill with `currentColor`. Icons are decorative (`aria-hidden`).

| Tool | Glyph |
|---|---|
| Select | Arrow cursor. |
| Select Box | Dashed rectangle plus a small cursor. |
| Pose | Bent limb of three dots plus a drag arrow. |
| Circle | Circle, r = 8. |
| Box | 16 × 12 rectangle, solid (not dashed) outline. |
| Polygon | Convex pentagon with vertex dots. |
| Hinge Joint | Two overlapping bars, a pin and a small arc. |

**States.**
- The active tool has `aria-pressed="true"`, every other button `"false"`; at most one button is pressed. Select is active on load, so its button is pressed and is the roving stop (Keyboard, below); New and Load keep the active tool, and a mode change that disables it activates Select ([§5.1](05-1-modes.md#51-editor-modes)). The active tool is not persisted.
- Tools that are not built yet, or that the current mode disables ([§5.1](05-1-modes.md#51-editor-modes)), use `aria-disabled` and stay focusable. Enabled state and reason come from the `tool:<id>` actions, exactly like header buttons: a tool whose implementation is not registered is disabled with "(not available yet)" in every mode; a built tool that the mode policy refuses is disabled with "(not available in *Mode* mode)". Until the tool registry is built into the editor, every button is disabled and none is pressed.
- Each button's accessible name is the tool label, and `aria-keyshortcuts` is its hotkey.
- **Tooltip:** "*Label* (*Key*)", built like the header tooltips ([§5.3](05-3-header.md#53-header)), plus " (not available yet)" or " (not available in *Mode* mode)" when disabled. It is a custom tooltip, shown after 400 ms of mouse hover or immediately when the button gets `:focus-visible` focus, and hidden on pointer leave, blur, a press and Esc. It sits 8 px to the right of its button, vertically centered, kept inside the window, above every panel. One tooltip shows at a time, and its text follows state changes while shown. It is hidden from assistive technology (`aria-hidden`); buttons have no `title` attribute.

**Keyboard.**
- `role="toolbar"`, labelled "Tools", `aria-orientation="vertical"`, one Tab stop. Up/Down and Left/Right both move through the buttons in their order (in either layout) and wrap, Home/End jump to the first/last; disabled buttons are included. Enter/Space activate the focused button, which keeps focus. Esc hides the tooltip. These keys do not trigger global hotkeys ([§5.9](05-9-hotkeys.md#59-hotkeys-defaults)).
- The roving stop is the active tool (the first button when none is active). While focus is inside the toolbar it stays on the focused button; when focus leaves the toolbar, the stop returns to the active tool.
- Tab order: inside the Viewport, Tools come before the viewport host; the Viewport takes its place in the layout order ([§5.2](05-2-panels.md#52-panels)). In the default layout this is header → Tools → viewport host → the other panels.
- A mouse click on an enabled button activates the tool and moves focus to the viewport host; on a disabled button it does nothing. Clicking the active tool again only moves focus. Tool hotkeys work wherever focus is, except in text fields.

**Common rules.**
- **Input.** Tools receive left and right presses on the viewport, and pointer moves over it with or without a button held. The middle button and Space + left drag navigate and never reach tools ([§5.4](05-4-viewport.md#54-viewport)); after a pan or zoom during a drag, the tool sees the pointer at its new world position.
- **Operations.** A left press, drag and release is one operation and at most one undo step ([§1](01-conventions.md#1-conventions)). A press and release that moved less than 4 px is a click; a second click within 400 ms and 4 px of the first is a double click. While a left drag is in progress, other buttons do nothing in the tool.
- **Modifiers** (SHIFT, CTRL, ALT; Cmd counts as CTRL) are read live: pressing or releasing one during a drag updates the operation without moving the pointer.
- **Aborting.** Esc, choosing another tool, Undo, a mode change, New or Load during a drag aborts it and leaves the document as it was ([§1](01-conventions.md#1-conventions)); so does the pointer being lost (window switch, pointer capture lost). Esc, choosing another tool, a mode change, New and Load also abort an operation that spans several clicks (a polygon being drawn); Undo between those clicks undoes the previous step and keeps the operation. Choosing the active tool again does nothing.
- **Coordinates and snapping.** Tools work in world meters and store root-local data. Points and lengths they place snap as [§5.4](05-4-viewport.md#54-viewport) says.
- **Creation tools** (Circle, Box, Polygon, Hinge Joint) show what they will create while drawing, change the document only when the operation completes (one undo step), then select what they created (the new shape, or the new joint) and stay active. An operation that is aborted or refused changes neither the document nor the selection.
- **Cursor:** the active tool's cursor (crosshair for creation tools and Select Box, the default arrow for Select, which follows what is under the pointer, below), except while navigating.

**Tools.**

- **Select and Select Box, common rules:**
  - They select world objects in the displayed pose ([§5.4](05-4-viewport.md#54-viewport)): shapes, joints and, by click only, the root. A body is never selected in the viewport: a click or a box takes its shapes, and the body itself is selected from the Hierarchy ([§5.6](05-6-hierarchy-assets-problems.md#56-hierarchy-assets-and-problems-panels)). Sprites and sprite assets are never selected here.
  - Selecting never changes the document and records no undo step; the selection is not undoable ([§1](01-conventions.md#1-conventions)). Both tools select in every mode. Select Box never changes the document; Select also moves, rotates and resizes the selection in Edit mode (below).
  - The selection changes only when the operation completes, at the release, except when a Select drag starts on an object outside the target (below). An aborted operation ([Common rules](#55-tools)) leaves the selection as it was.
  - Once the pointer has moved 4 px from the press point on screen, the press is a drag until its release, even if the pointer comes back (as for Circle and Box).
  - **Selection box:** a drag draws a rectangle from the press point to the pointer, axis-aligned on screen, in `#5b9cf5` (1 px outline, 10 % fill). The press point is held as a world point, so it stays on the same spot of the world when the view pans or zooms during the drag. Box corners are not snapped. At the release the box takes every shape that lies **entirely inside** it and every joint whose anchor lies inside it, edges included. The root is never taken by a box. Shapes with invalid geometry and joints whose anchor is not placed are skipped, as in hit testing; shapes hidden by the display mode are taken.
  - **Box modifiers**, read at the release: none replaces the selection with the boxed objects (an empty box clears the selection); SHIFT adds them; CTRL removes them. CTRL wins when both are held; ALT does nothing.
- **Select** (`V`): click to select one object, drag an object to move it, drag empty space for a selection box. In Edit mode the selection also carries a **transform gizmo** to move it along an axis, move it freely or rotate it, and every selected circle or box, and every circle and box of a selected body, carries its own **resize handles**: the gizmo belongs to the bodies, resize handles to each shape. A single selected joint carries **limit handles** on the gizmo's ring (Joint limits, below). Everything works in the displayed pose ([§5.4](05-4-viewport.md#54-viewport)).
  - **Press.** A left press is resolved in this order:
    1. on a handle of the selection (*Handles and priority*, below; in Animate mode only the rotation ring, in Simulate mode none): a **handle press**;
    2. on a hit ([§5.4](05-4-viewport.md#54-viewport), Hit testing): an **object press**;
    3. on empty space: a **box press**.

    The root never moves ([§2.1](02-object-model.md#21-root-the-ragdoll-itself)): an object press and its drag use the hits without the root, and a press whose only hit is the root is an object press for clicks (it selects the root) and draws a selection box when it becomes a drag.

    Right presses do nothing. A handle press that never becomes a drag does nothing; object and box presses that never become a drag are clicks, below.
  - A **click** looks at the hits at the press point, topmost first ([§5.4](05-4-viewport.md#54-viewport), Hit testing). Its **target** is the topmost hit. With ALT, the target is the hit just below the topmost hit that is already selected, wrapping to the topmost; when no hit is selected, it is the topmost. Repeated ALT + clicks therefore cycle through overlapping objects. Example: with the hits [joint, shape A, shape B] and an empty selection, ALT + clicks select the joint, then A, then B, then the joint again.
  - **Click modifiers**, read at the release: none selects only the target; SHIFT adds the target, or removes it when it is already selected; CTRL removes it. CTRL wins over SHIFT; ALT only chooses the target and combines with both.
  - A click on empty space clears the selection when no modifier is held (as Esc does), and does nothing with SHIFT or CTRL.
  - Hover keeps showing the topmost hit, also while ALT is held. A double click is two clicks.
  - **Drag.** A box press draws a selection box (common rules above). An object press, in Edit mode, **moves** (below): when the topmost hit belongs to the target (it is selected, or it is a shape whose body is in the target), the whole target moves; otherwise, once the press becomes a drag, the topmost hit is selected alone and its target moves. A drag always starts from the topmost hit: ALT only chooses a click's target. SHIFT and CTRL never change what a drag selects. The selection made when the drag starts is not part of the operation, so an aborted drag keeps it. In Animate and Simulate mode an object press that becomes a drag draws a selection box, like a box press. A handle press drags its handle.
  - **Target.** The selection decides what moves and rotates: every selected body, the body of every selected shape, and every selected joint. The root, sprite assets and clips are ignored, so with only the root selected the target is empty and no gizmo is shown.
  - **Move** (an object drag, a gizmo arrow or the gizmo square):
    - **Bodies:** each target body moves with its **subtree** ([§2.4](02-object-model.md#24-joints)), so moving an upper arm carries the forearm and the hand. Each target joint that is the parent joint of a body (its **child body**) moves that body's subtree the same way, so moving the elbow carries the forearm and the hand; a joint that is no body's parent joint (a loop joint, or one without valid bodies) has no child body. ALT moves only the target bodies and no child body. Each body moves once, also when subtrees overlap.
    - **Joint anchors** move with the bodies when the body they are the parent joint of moves, or when both their bodies move; the others stay. Selected joints move their anchor too, so with ALT, or with only joints that have no child body, a drag moves just the target joints' anchors. Example: moving an upper arm carries the shoulder, elbow and wrist anchors; ALT + dragging the forearm carries the elbow anchor and leaves the wrist anchor with the hand; dragging the elbow joint moves the elbow and wrist anchors with the forearm and the hand, and ALT + dragging it moves only the elbow anchor. Validation warns when an anchor ends outside both bodies' shapes ([§6.4](06-file-format.md#64-validation)).
    - Everything moves by one vector, in world axes: the pointer's displacement since the press, adjusted so that a **reference point** lands on the grid when grid snap is on ([§5.4](05-4-viewport.md#54-viewport)). The reference point is the pressed object's point for an object drag (the origin of the pressed shape's body, the pressed joint's anchor) and the pivot for a gizmo drag. The X arrow keeps the vector's Y at 0 and snaps only the reference point's X; the Y arrow the reverse.
  - **Transform gizmo.** Drawn in Edit mode while Select is active, the target is not empty and its pivot (below) is placed, also during drags (it follows the pivot), but not while a selection box is drawn. It sits on the pivot, has a constant size in CSS px at every zoom, and stays aligned with the world axes: it never turns with the root or the bodies.
    - **X arrow**, `#e0524f` (the grid's X axis color): a 2 px shaft from 16 px to 46 px right of the pivot, then a filled head 8 px wide whose tip is 56 px from the pivot. Dragging it moves the target along world X only.
    - **Y arrow**, `#58b85c` (the grid's Y axis color): the same shape, pointing up. Dragging it moves the target along world Y only.
    - **Square**, `#f2c94c`: a 12 × 12 px square between the arrows, from 16 to 28 px right of and above the pivot, filled at 40 % opacity with a 1 px outline. Dragging it moves the target freely.
    - **Rotation ring**, `#3d6dff`: a dashed 2 px circle (32 dashes, half dash and half gap, constant on screen) of 72 px radius around the pivot, enclosing the arrows. Dragging anywhere on it rotates the target (Rotation, below). It is shown only when a rotation can change something: the target holds a body, or holds two or more joints, or it is one joint that has a child body (Move, above) or whose limits can be rotated (Joint limits, below). When the limits can be rotated, dragging the ring rotates the limit range instead, also when the joint has a child body.
    - **Pivot mark**, `#d8dade`, at the pivot: a cross with 4 px arms in Center mode, a 4 px ring with a 1.5 px dot in its center in Last selected mode (Pivot, below). It shows the pivot mode and is not a handle.
    - **Hit areas:** the square grown by 2 px on every side; an arrow within 6 px of the segment from 16 px to its tip; the ring within 6 px of its circle. No point within 12 px of the pivot (the root's hit radius) belongs to the gizmo, so a joint or the root drawn at the pivot is still pressed as an object (the root only to be clicked).
    - **Hover:** the handle under the pointer, and the handle being dragged, is drawn in `#ffffff` with its stroke 1 px wider. While the pointer is over a handle, no object hover is shown ([§5.4](05-4-viewport.md#54-viewport), Feedback).
    - **Readout:** during every move, rotation, resize or limit drag of Select (object, gizmo, resize or limit handle), a text 16 px right of and 16 px below the pointer, kept inside the viewport, in the style of the name labels (11 px, `#d8dade`, dark outline, [§5.4](05-4-viewport.md#54-viewport)). A move shows `Δx: 12.0 px  Δy: -3.5 px`: the vector applied since the press, in world axes and in document pixels (`units.pixelsPerMeter`, [§1](01-conventions.md#1-conventions)), not screen pixels. A rotation shows `Δθ: 15.0°`: the rotation applied since the press, counter-clockwise positive. A resize shows the resulting size in document pixels: `w: 50.0 px  h: 25.0 px` for a box, `r: 12.5 px` for a circle. A limit drag shows the resulting limits: `lower: -30.0°  upper: 45.0°`. One decimal; `-0.0` is shown as `0.0`.
  - **Pivot.** The point the target rotates around and the gizmo sits on, from the displayed pose:
    - **One object:** for one body (any number of its shapes selected), the anchor of its parent joint ([§2.4](02-object-model.md#24-joints)) when it has one and that anchor is placed, otherwise the body origin; for one joint, its anchor.
    - **Several objects** (bodies and joints of the target), by the **pivot mode**: **Center** (default) takes the average of their points, the origin of every target body and the placed anchor of every target joint; **Last selected** takes the pivot the last selected of them would have alone. The last selected one is the target object that the last entry of the selection order maps to (a shape maps to its body).
    - **CTRL** forces the body origin wherever a parent joint anchor would be used (one body, or a last selected body); in Center mode it changes nothing.
    - **Z** switches the pivot mode between Center and Last selected, in every mode, but not during a drag. The pivot mode is an editor setting ([§5.3](05-3-header.md#53-header)) and the pivot mark shows it.
    - The pivot follows the target during a move and stays fixed during a rotation.
  - **Rotation** (dragging the ring) rotates by the angle the pointer sweeps around the pivot since the press, following it past half a turn. While it is dragged, a translucent slice of the ring's circle (`#3d6dff` at 25 % fill, 1 px outline, ring radius, constant on screen) is drawn from the press direction to the applied angle, after SHIFT rounding, turning the same way as the target on screen; a sweep of 360° or more fills the whole disc while the readout keeps the total.
    - **Bodies and joints:** everything a move would carry rotates around the pivot: every target body with its subtree, the subtree of every target joint's child body, the carried anchors and every target joint's anchor; ALT rotates only the target bodies and the anchors of the target joints. Example: rotating the elbow joint alone turns the forearm and the hand around the elbow anchor; with ALT nothing changes, since the anchor is the pivot.
    - SHIFT rounds to 15° ([§5.4](05-4-viewport.md#54-viewport)): the body's root-local `angle` when the target is one body; otherwise the rotation itself. CTRL, ALT and SHIFT are read live; when CTRL changes the pivot during a drag, the swept angle is measured again from the press point around the new pivot.
  - **Handles and priority.** In Edit mode, a press or hover is tested against the selection's handles before hit testing ([§5.4](05-4-viewport.md#54-viewport)), and the first match wins:
    1. the gizmo square;
    2. the **point handles** of the selection: the limit handles, the `aimAxis` tip, sprite handles, resize handles, each with an 8 px hit radius around its center; when several are in reach, the one whose center is nearest wins, and on a tie the first in this list (limit handles: lower, then upper; resize handles: by shape in resize order, below, then within a shape corners, then sides, then the circle handle);
    3. the X arrow, then the Y arrow;
    4. the rotation ring.

    Handles are drawn in the reverse order, so the handle on top is the one a press takes. Joints, the root and shapes come after every handle, except within 12 px of the pivot, where the gizmo has no hit area and a joint or the root under the pointer wins over point handles (a point handle there is taken when neither is hit), so the joint or root at the pivot stays pressable. The gizmo never moves to make room for point handles. An object hidden under a handle is reached by zooming in (handles keep their size while the objects spread apart) or from the Hierarchy.
  - **`aimAxis` handle:** when the target is exactly one joint, the tip of its `aimAxis` arrow ([§5.4](05-4-viewport.md#54-viewport)) is a point handle. Dragging it points `aimAxis` from the anchor toward the pointer, relative to bodyB's angle; SHIFT rounds `aimAxis` to 15°.
  - **Joint limits:** when the target is exactly one joint (no body) that has a zero direction ([§5.4](05-4-viewport.md#54-viewport), Gizmos: valid `bodyA` and `bodyB`, a placed anchor) and finite limits with `lower ≤ 0 ≤ upper`, the gizmo edits its limits ([§2.4](02-object-model.md#24-joints)). A joint stores no angle of its own, so this is its rotation handling: the ring turns the limit range, and the two ends move apart. Shown whether `limits.enabled` is on or off.
    - **Look**, around the anchor (the pivot) at the ring radius, constant on screen, angles counter-clockwise from the zero direction and turning with the root: a **reference line**, 1 px `#d8dade`, from the joint circle (6 px) to the ring along the zero direction; a **range wedge** from `lower` to `upper`, `#3d6dff` at 15 % fill; a **range arc**, 2 px solid `#3d6dff` on the ring from `lower` to `upper`; and two **limit handles**, circles of 5 px radius at the arc ends, filled `#d8dade` with a 1 px `#1b1d21` outline, `#ffffff` and 6 px radius under the pointer or while dragged. With limits disabled, wedge, arc and handles are drawn at 50 % opacity. They draw under the arrows, the handles with the other point handles (Handles and priority).
    - The reference line is not a handle: the zero direction is the root's +X at rest ([§5.4](05-4-viewport.md#54-viewport), Gizmos), whatever the bodies' positions, so it turns only with the root.
    - **Limit handle drag:** the limit becomes its press value plus the angle the pointer sweeps around the anchor since the press (measured as for Rotation, past half a turn), SHIFT rounds it to 15° ([§5.4](05-4-viewport.md#54-viewport)), then it is clamped: `lower` to [min(−180°, `lower` at the press), 0], `upper` to [0, max(180°, `upper` at the press)]. The other limit stays. When `lower = upper = 0` the handles coincide: the drag takes `upper` once the sweep first turns counter-clockwise, `lower` once it first turns clockwise, and keeps that limit until the release; until then nothing changes.
    - **ALT** (symmetry, as in Hinge Joint creation): while held, the other limit is the mirror of the dragged one (other = −dragged), whatever both limits were at the press, zero or not, then clamped to its own interval above (so each stays on its side of 0 and within ±180°, and one already beyond ±180° is never pushed further). With SHIFT the dragged limit is rounded to 15° first and the other mirrors it. With coincident 0 / 0 handles both open symmetrically, the first sweep direction picking the dragged handle. ALT is read live: releasing it puts the other limit back to its press value. ALT does nothing on the ring.
    - **Ring drag** (rotate the range): both limits move by δ, the swept angle rounded to 15° with SHIFT, then clamped to [max(−`upper₀`, min(0, −180° − `lower₀`)), min(−`lower₀`, max(0, 180° − `upper₀`))] (press values), so the width is kept, 0 stays inside the range and no limit passes ±180° (one already beyond it can only come back). The ring is shown for one joint only when that interval holds more than 0 (not for 0 / 0, nor for −180° / 180°). No rotation slice is drawn; the wedge shows the result.
    - **Result:** `limits.lower`, `limits.upper` and `limits.enabled` = true, one undo step. A drag that leaves both limits at their press values changes nothing, `limits.enabled` included. The anchor and the bodies never change.
    - Example: limits −30° / 45°. Dragging the upper handle 20° clockwise gives −30° / 25°; with SHIFT, −30° / 30°; with ALT, −25° / 25°. From −20° / 70°, dragging the upper handle to 50° with ALT gives −50° / 50°. Dragging the ring 50° counter-clockwise gives 0° / 75° (δ clamped to 30°); 20° clockwise gives −50° / 25°.
  - **Resize handles:** shown in Edit mode while the gizmo is shown, on every **selected shape** and every shape of a **selected body**, when it is a circle or a box with valid geometry ([§5.4](05-4-viewport.md#54-viewport), Hit testing). So a selected shape shows handles only on itself (not on the other shapes of its body, although the gizmo still moves and rotates the whole body), a selected body shows them on each of its shapes, and a multi-selection shows the union. Polygons and invalid shapes show none; a selected joint or root adds none. The **resize order** follows the selection order: a selected shape takes its own place, a selected body its shapes in child order, each shape once at its first place; it breaks handle ties (Handles and priority, above).
    - **One shape at a time:** dragging a handle resizes only the shape it belongs to, by the rules below; every other shape, selected or not, stays as it is. Scaling several shapes together (group or proportional resize) is not offered.
    - **Look:** 8 × 8 px squares, axis-aligned on screen, filled `#d8dade` (the neutral gizmo color, distinct from the gizmo's red, green, yellow and blue) with a 1 px `#1b1d21` outline; the handle under the pointer, and the one being dragged, is filled `#ffffff` and drawn 10 × 10 px. Constant size at every zoom; drawn above the shape outlines and the arrows, below the gizmo square.
    - **Box:** 8 handles, at the 4 corners and the 4 side midpoints of the box as displayed, so they turn with the root, the body and the shape's own `angle`. Resizing works in the box's own axes (its width along its +X, its height along its +Y). A corner changes both sizes, a side handle only the size across its side. By default the opposite corner or side stays fixed; ALT or CTRL keeps the box center fixed instead and resizes both ways (as the Box tool does); SHIFT keeps the aspect ratio of the press: both sizes scale by the larger of the factors of the axes the handle changes, and a side handle with SHIFT grows the other axis about the box's center line. Handles never cross the fixed side: the box stops at the minimum size.
    - **Circle:** one handle on the circumference, along the circle's +X (the body's +X turned by the root, so on the right side at angle 0). Dragging it sets the radius to the distance from the center to the handle point; the center stays. No modifiers.
    - **Handle point and snapping:** the dragged handle point is the handle's position at the press plus the pointer's displacement since the press, so grabbing a handle off its center does not jump. With grid snap on, a box's handle point snaps to the grid ([§5.4](05-4-viewport.md#54-viewport)) before it is taken into the box's axes, and a circle's radius is rounded to the minor spacing, as the Circle tool does.
    - **Minimum size:** width, height and radius never go below 0.01 m, nor below the minor spacing with grid snap on.
    - **Result:** only the shape's geometry changes: `shape.center`, `shape.width` and `shape.height` for a box, `shape.radius` for a circle. The body's `position` and `angle`, the shape's `angle` and every joint stay as they are. The gizmo stays at the pivot it had at the press.

    With `q` the snapped handle point in the box's axes relative to the box center at the press, `w0 × h0` the size at the press and `s` the handle's outward sign per axis (+1, −1, or 0 for the axis a side handle leaves alone): along each axis the handle changes, the size is `max(min, s·q + size0 / 2)` with the opposite side fixed and the center moved by `s·(size − size0) / 2`, or `max(min, 2·s·q)` with ALT or CTRL and the center kept; with SHIFT, `k` = the largest `size / size0` among the changed axes, raised when needed so that no `k·size0` is below the minimum, every axis takes `k·size0`, a changed axis keeps its fixed side (or the center with ALT or CTRL) and an unchanged one keeps the center. Example: a 2 × 1 m box centered at (0, 0), angle 0, its top-right corner dragged from (1, 0.5) to (2, 0.5): 3 × 1 centered at (0.5, 0); with SHIFT, 3 × 1.5 centered at (0.5, 0.25); with ALT, 4 × 1 at (0, 0); with ALT and SHIFT, 4 × 2 at (0, 0). Its right side handle dragged from (1, 0) to (2, 0.7): 3 × 1 at (0.5, 0); with SHIFT, 3 × 1.5 at (0.5, 0).
  - **Sprite edit** (toggle `S` while a shape with a sprite is selected): the gizmo and the handles edit the sprite instead of the shape: the arrows and the square change `offset`, the ring changes `angle` around the sprite's center, the corner handles change `scale` (SHIFT keeps proportions). Physics is untouched. The resize handles are not shown during sprite edit.
  - **Cursor** in Edit mode: `move` over the square or an object (the default arrow when the root is the only hit, since it never moves), `ew-resize` over the X arrow, `ns-resize` over the Y arrow, `pointer` over the ring and the other point handles, over a resize handle the CSS resize cursor (`ew-resize`, `nesw-resize`, `ns-resize`, `nwse-resize`) closest to its outward direction on screen, the default arrow over empty space; during a drag, the cursor of what was pressed (`move` for an object). In Animate mode, `pointer` over the ring and the default arrow elsewhere; in Simulate mode, the default arrow.
  - **Undo:** each move, rotation, resize or limit drag is one undo step, and a drag that ends where it started leaves none ([§1](01-conventions.md#1-conventions)). Clicks and selection boxes record nothing.
  - **In Animate mode** only rotation is offered, into keys. The gizmo shows only the rotation ring, and only when the target is exactly one body that has a parent joint, whose anchor is the pivot; CTRL, ALT and the pivot mode do nothing. Rotating sets the `angle` of that parent joint at the playhead (keyed if autokey is on), and the subtree follows by FK. Clicks and selection boxes select as in Edit mode, an object drag draws a selection box, and nothing moves.
  - **In Simulate mode** Select only selects: no gizmo, and every drag draws a selection box.
- **Select Box** (`B`): drag for a selection box. A press that never becomes a drag acts as an empty box: without modifiers it clears the selection, with them it does nothing. It never moves anything and shows no gizmo, so a box can start on top of objects, which a Select drag would move. Shapes of several bodies selected this way can be grouped into one body (Ctrl+G), and shapes of one body split out of it (Alt+G) ([§2.2](02-object-model.md#22-bodies-and-shapes)).
- **Pose** (`D`): simulates physics while the mouse is held, with **no gravity**. The grabbed body is pulled by a mouse joint toward the pointer. Joints have **no motors** (motors belong to animation): they are free hinges within their limits. Every body gets high damping during the drag, so bodies that are not pulled stop as soon as the pointer stops. The ragdoll never collides with itself during the test.
  - **Press:** a left press on a shape grabs its body at the pointer (unsnapped; joints and the root are skipped, the topmost shape hit wins). A press on empty space, a right press and modifiers do nothing. Pose never changes the selection.
  - **Drag:** the simulation starts at the press and runs at a fixed step while the button is held, also when the pointer does not move. The viewport shows the simulated pose: shapes, joint gizmos and hover follow it, and a line in the neutral gizmo color joins the grabbed point on the body to the pointer.
  - **Refused:** when the document has validation errors ([§6.4](06-file-format.md#64-validation); clips and sprites are not part of the test and never refuse it) the press starts nothing and the cursor is `not-allowed` until the release.
  - **Cursor:** `grab` over a shape, `grabbing` during a drag, the default arrow elsewhere.
  - **End:** the release, Esc, a tool switch, a mode change, or anything else that cancels the press ([§5.1](05-1-modes.md#51-editor-modes)) ends the test and shows the rest pose again.
  - In **Edit mode** the result is discarded on release (testing only). It never overrides the rest pose, never changes the document and records no undo step.
  - In **Animate mode** the result becomes the FK pose at the playhead. With **autokey** on, `angle` keys are created for every joint whose angle changed by more than 0.1°.
- **Circle and Box, common rules:**
  - The press point, grid-snapped ([§5.4](05-4-viewport.md#54-viewport)), is the **anchor**. Once the pointer has moved 4 px from the press point on screen, the press is a drag until its release, even if the pointer comes back.
  - A **click** (a press and release that never became a drag) creates the shape at its default size, centered at the anchor, whatever modifiers are held: a circle of radius 0.25 m, a 0.5 × 0.5 m box ([`docs/schema.md`](../schema.md)). This default shape is the preview until the press becomes a drag.
  - A drag that ends with a radius, width or height of 0 creates nothing (refused).
  - The new shape gets a new body placed as [§2.2](02-object-model.md#22-bodies-and-shapes) says, so it keeps the on-screen orientation it was drawn with: the box is axis-aligned on screen even when the root is rotated. The body is named `body_<n>` and the shape `circle_<n>` or `box_<n>`, each `<n>` being the number of its own id (`b_3` → `body_3`, `f_5` → `circle_5`). The first body created while `rootBody` is `null` becomes `rootBody` ([§2.1](02-object-model.md#21-root-the-ragdoll-itself)).
- **Circle** (`C`): press to place the center at the anchor, drag to set the radius, release to create. The radius is the distance from the anchor to the pointer (not snapped as a point), rounded to the minor grid spacing and never below it when grid snap is on ([§5.4](05-4-viewport.md#54-viewport)). No modifiers. Esc aborts.
- **Box** (`R`): press to place the anchor, drag to define the rectangle against the grid-snapped pointer, release to create. By default the anchor is the corner opposite the pointer, so it can be any of the four corners, and the final size is never negative. Modifiers combine:
  - SHIFT enforces a square: both sides take the longer one, growing away from the anchor.
  - ALT enforces symmetry in both axes: the box is centered on the anchor.
  - CTRL puts the anchor at the center, the same effect as ALT. ALT and CTRL together are the same as either one.
  - Esc aborts.

  With `d` the vector from the anchor to the snapped pointer: both axes are *centered* when ALT or CTRL is held, else both are *edge-anchored*. The size along an axis is `2·|d|` when centered, else `|d|`; SHIFT then sets both sizes to the larger one. A centered axis keeps the anchor as the box center; an edge-anchored one puts the center at `anchor + sign(d)·size / 2`, with `sign(0) = +1`. Example: anchor (0, 0), pointer (2, 1) gives center (1, 0.5) and size 2 × 1; with ALT, CTRL or both, center (0, 0) and 4 × 2; with SHIFT, center (1, 1) and 2 × 2; with SHIFT and ALT or CTRL, center (0, 0) and 4 × 4.
- **Polygon** (`N`):
  - Click adds a vertex linked to the previous one.
  - Double click closes the shape (last vertex to first).
  - Right click deletes the last vertex.
  - Esc aborts.
  - The shape must be convex with at most 8 vertices. Invalid edges are drawn red while drawing, and closing an invalid polygon is refused.
- **Hinge Joint** (`H`): press where shapes of two different bodies overlap to join those bodies with a revolute joint whose anchor is the press point.
  - **Pair.** At the press, the shapes under the pointer (hit testing of [§5.4](05-4-viewport.md#54-viewport); joints and the root are skipped) are walked topmost first, and the first two different bodies they belong to are joined. bodyA and bodyB are assigned by the rule of [§2.4](02-object-model.md#24-joints). The bodies come from the pointer position, not from the snapped anchor.
  - **Refused presses.** A press does nothing (no joint, no undo step, selection unchanged) when fewer than two bodies are under the pointer (empty space, a single shape, or only shapes of one body), or when a joint already connects those two bodies, in either order. While no press is in progress, the cursor is `not-allowed` wherever a press would be refused, and the crosshair elsewhere.
  - **Anchor.** The press point, grid-snapped ([§5.4](05-4-viewport.md#54-viewport)), stored root-local in the rest pose. With grid snap on it may fall outside both shapes; validation reports that as a warning ([§6.4](06-file-format.md#64-validation)).
  - **Click** (a press and release that never became a drag, with the same 4 px latch as Circle and Box): creates a **pin**, a joint with limits disabled.
  - **Drag:** creates **one-sided** limits, enabled: point where the child may swing to. The limit angle φ is the angle from the zero direction (the root's +X, [§5.4](05-4-viewport.md#54-viewport)) to the direction from the anchor to the pointer, counter-clockwise positive, in (−180°, 180°], rounded to a whole degree; SHIFT rounds it to a multiple of 15° ([§5.4](05-4-viewport.md#54-viewport)). φ > 0 gives `lower` 0 and `upper` φ; φ < 0 gives `lower` φ and `upper` 0. **ALT** makes them symmetric, ±|φ|. The distance to the anchor does not matter, and with the pointer within 1 px of the anchor φ keeps its last value (0 at first). The direction is measured from where the anchor is on screen at that moment, so panning or zooming during the drag keeps φ consistent with the preview. A drag that ends with φ = 0 creates a pin. CTRL does nothing. Example: with the root not rotated, a drag up and to the right at 45° gives 0° / 45°, with ALT −45° / 45°; down and to the right gives −45° / 0°; straight up gives 0° / 90° and straight down −90° / 0°, wherever bodyB lies.
  - **Preview** while pressed, in the hover color at 1 px: the joint gizmo of [§5.4](05-4-viewport.md#54-viewport) at the anchor, i.e. its circle, its needle along the zero direction (the root's +X) and, while φ ≠ 0, the limit arc from `lower` to `upper`. During a drag a readout shows the limits as Select's limit drags do (`lower: 0.0°  upper: 45.0°`), placed like Select's readout.
  - **Result:** one new joint with the schema defaults ([`docs/schema.md`](../schema.md)) for every other field (motor, `aimAxis` 0, `collideConnected` false), named `joint_<n>` after its id (`j_4` → `joint_4`), appended to `joints`. `rootBody` is not changed. The joint is selected and the tool stays active. A joint that closes a loop in the joint graph is allowed and becomes a loop joint ([§2.4](02-object-model.md#24-joints)).
  - The right button does nothing. Esc aborts. Limits are refined afterwards with the Select tool's limit handles (Joint limits, above) or in the inspector, like every other joint field.
