# 5.4 Viewport
- **Camera:** a `center` in meters and a `scale` in CSS px per meter, independent of `pixelsPerMeter`. Screen coordinates are CSS px from the top-left corner of the viewport host, Y down:

  ```
  screen.x = (x − center.x) · scale + width / 2
  screen.y = (center.y − y) · scale + height / 2        screen angle = −angle
  ```

  - `scale` is clamped to 2–10 000 px/m, and each coordinate of `center` to ±10 000 m.
  - Changing `pixelsPerMeter` keeps the camera: shapes stay in place on screen and sprites change size ([§1](01-conventions.md#1-conventions)).
  - Resizing the viewport keeps `center` and `scale`.
  - The camera is not saved and not undoable. After New and draft recovery, and when the editor starts, the view frames everything; Load leaves the camera as it was.
- **Navigation** works in every mode and never reaches the active tool. A press on the canvas focuses the viewport host.
  - **Pan:** middle-button drag, or Space + left drag while the viewport has focus. The world point under the pointer follows it. The cursor is `grab`, and `grabbing` while dragging. Panning or zooming during a tool gesture does not end the gesture; a middle press during a left drag pans too. Known limitation, pending [§12](12-open-items.md#12-open-items--to-verify): if the browser takes pointer capture away on that middle press, the drag is aborted like any other lost capture ([§5.5](05-5-tools.md#55-tools)) and the document is left as it was.
  - **Zoom:** the mouse wheel zooms around the pointer, keeping the world point under it fixed: `scale · 2^(−Δy / 400)`, with Δy in px (line deltas × 16, page deltas × the viewport height). A wheel with Ctrl (trackpad pinch) uses `2^(−Δy / 100)`. A wheel over the viewport never scrolls or zooms the page. With the viewport focused, `+` (or `=`) and `-` zoom by √2 around the viewport center, and keep zooming while held (they are navigation keys, not [§5.9](05-9-hotkeys.md#59-hotkeys-defaults) hotkeys).
  - **Framing:** `F` frames the selection, or everything when the selection has nothing to frame; `Home` frames everything. The framed box is the world bounding box of the shapes as displayed (the displayed pose, below), the joint anchors (in Animate, each anchor moves with its `bodyA`) and the root's position. Of the selection, a body counts its shapes, a shape itself, a joint its anchor and the root its position; other objects count nothing. Sprite images are not included. The box is grown around its center to at least 1 × 1 m (4 × 4 m around the root when the ragdoll has no bodies and no joints), then fitted as large as the scale limits allow into the viewport minus insets of 16 px at the top, right and bottom and, at the left, the Tools overlay width plus 16 px, so nothing framed sits under the overlay. Framing is instant.
- **Grid:** world-space lines behind everything, with the X axis (`y = 0`) in red and the Y axis (`x = 0`) in green over them. Its major spacing is the *grid size* setting (default 1 m) and its minor spacing that size divided by *subdivisions* (default 10, so 0.1 m) ([§5.3](05-3-header.md#53-header)). *Show grid* hides it, axes included.
  - **Levels:** the minor spacing, the major spacing, then the major spacing × 10ⁿ for n ≥ 1, equal spacings counted once. At most two levels are drawn: the finest level whose lines are at least 8 px apart on screen, whose opacity ramps from 0 at 8 px to full at 32 px, and the next coarser level, drawn stronger. Zooming out therefore turns 1 m lines into the fine level under 10 m lines.
  - Lines are 1 CSS px wide at every zoom, aligned to device pixels.
- **Snapping:**
  - **Grid snap** (*Snap to grid*, default off) rounds the points that tools place or drag to the nearest multiple of the minor spacing, in world coordinates, whether the grid is shown or not: creation points and polygon vertices, the Hinge anchor, and the point a Select move carries: the pressed object's point when an object is dragged (body origin with its subtree following, joint anchor), the pivot when the transform gizmo is dragged; an arrow of the gizmo snaps only its own axis; the handle point of a box resize handle ([§5.5](05-5-tools.md#55-tools)). Sizes follow from snapped points, and a circle's radius (Circle tool and circle resize handle) is rounded to the minor spacing, never below it. Sprite edit, Pose, inspector fields and Hierarchy additions do not snap.
  - **Angle snap:** SHIFT while rotating (Select rotation ring, `aimAxis` handle, sprite edit ring), while dragging a joint's limit handles or limit range, or while dragging Hinge Joint limits rounds the resulting angle to a multiple of 15°: the body's root-local `angle` when the target is one body, or else the rotation itself (several bodies, joints), `aimAxis` and the sprite `angle` in their own frames, the dragged limit for a limit handle, the range shift δ for a limit range, and the Hinge limit angle φ ([§5.5](05-5-tools.md#55-tools)).
- **Display modes** (Settings, default Both), in every editor mode:

  | Mode | Sprites | Shapes |
  |---|---|---|
  | Shapes | Hidden | Filled with `editor.color` at 40 % opacity, outlined in orange `#f08a24` |
  | Sprites | Shown | Hidden, except hover and selection outlines |
  | Both | Shown | Outlined in orange `#f08a24` at 60 % opacity and filled with ditor.color at 20 % opacity, over the sprites |

  Outlines are 1 CSS px wide at every zoom (selection outlines 2 px, below). Hit testing does not depend on the display mode, so a hidden shape can still be hovered and selected. A sprite whose image is missing is drawn as a placeholder whenever sprites are shown.
- **Displayed pose.** Gizmos, feedback, overlays and hit testing all follow the pose the viewport shows: the rest pose in Edit mode, in Animate mode the main figure of the Animate view (clip simulation, or FK pose while Pose is active, [§5.1](05-1-modes.md#51-editor-modes)), the live bodies in Simulate mode. A joint anchor moves with its `bodyA`.
- **Ghosts** (Animate mode, [§5.1](05-1-modes.md#51-editor-modes)): each ghost draws every shape with valid geometry at its pose, filled at 12 % opacity and outlined at 1 px at 50 % opacity, in green `#58b85c` (key time after the playhead) or red `#e0524f` (before it), the grid's axis colors, not the validation red. They are drawn above the grid and below the main figure's sprites and shapes, in every display mode, and nothing else of them is drawn.
- **Gizmos** are drawn above the shapes and sprites, at a constant size in CSS px at every zoom, joints above the root:
  - root: a pivot cross (14 px arms, turned by the root's angle) and a ring (10 px radius) at the root position;
  - joints: a circle (6 px radius) at the anchor; when limits are enabled, an arc (20 px radius) from `lower` to `upper`; a needle (20 px) at the current joint angle (0 in Edit mode); the strength fill in Animate mode; and, when the joint is selected, an `aimAxis` arrow (32 px) along bodyB's angle plus `aimAxis`. Arc and needle angles are measured from the joint's **zero direction**: the root's +X axis in the rest pose (pointing right when the root is not rotated), turned with bodyA's rotation since rest. It never depends on where bodyA or bodyB lie, so limits read as plain angles from a fixed reference: 90° is straight up from the anchor at rest. At rest the needle lies along that direction, and the arc shows how far bodyB may turn each way. Editor display only: limits keep their meaning, bodyB's rotation relative to bodyA since rest. A joint without a valid `bodyA` or `bodyB` draws only its circle, and one whose `anchor` is missing or not finite draws nothing, is never hit and is left out of framing;
  - the Select tool's transform gizmo, with the limit editor of a single selected joint, and its point handles (limit handles, `aimAxis` tip, sprite handles in sprite-edit mode, resize handles) ([§5.5](05-5-tools.md#55-tools)), drawn by the active tool above every other gizmo.

  Gizmos are drawn in `#d8dade`, except as the feedback below says.
- **Hit testing** decides what the pointer is over, for hover, the tools and drops onto the viewport. At a screen point the hits are, topmost first: joints whose anchor is within 8 px (later joints in `joints` order first), the root when its position is within 12 px, then shapes that contain the point or lie within 4 px of it (later bodies first, and later shapes of a body first). Bodies, sprite images and sprite assets are never hit: a click on a shape selects the shape, and its body is selected from the Hierarchy. Shapes with invalid geometry (a non-positive size, fewer than 3 vertices) are skipped; concave and self-intersecting polygon drafts are hit as drawn (even-odd rule). Handles of the active tool (the Select tool's gizmo and point handles, [§5.5](05-5-tools.md#55-tools)) are tested by the tool before these hits and win over them.
- **Feedback**, in every display mode, drawn over the shape outlines:
  - **Hover:** the first hit under the pointer is highlighted in `#5b9cf5` at 1 px: a shape's outline, or the root's or a joint's gizmo. Hover is shown while no button is pressed and no navigation is in progress, is cleared when the pointer leaves the viewport host (the Tools overlay included), and is not shown while a creation tool or Select Box is active, nor while the pointer is over a handle of the active tool (the handle highlights instead).
  - **Selection:** selected shapes, and every shape of a selected body, are outlined at 2 px in `#5b9cf5`; a selected root or joint draws its gizmo in that color, and a selected joint adds its `aimAxis` arrow. Selection wins over hover.
  - **Invalid:** objects that validation ([§6.4](06-file-format.md#64-validation)) reports with an error draw their outline (shapes; for a body, all its shapes; for a clip, track, key or event, nothing) or their gizmo (root, joints) in `#e5484d`. Red wins over the hover and selection colors; a selected invalid object keeps the 2 px width.
- **Overlays** are editor settings (Settings → Overlays, [§5.3](05-3-header.md#53-header)), all off by default, drawn above the gizmos at a constant size:
  - **Names:** the root's name above and to the right of its gizmo, each body's name centered just below its origin (so it never hides a center-of-mass mark there), and each joint's name above and to the right of its anchor, as 11 px text with a dark outline. An object whose name is empty shows its id. Labels may overlap.
  - **Centers of mass:** a 5 px quartered circle at each body's center of mass, computed as Box2D does (area × density of every shape, sensors included; a concave polygon draft, which validation rejects, counts its own area). A body whose mass is 0 shows none; shapes with invalid geometry or a negative density add nothing.
  - **Contacts** (Simulate mode only): a dot at each contact point of the live world.
- **Rename popup** (F2 outside the Hierarchy, [§5.6](05-6-hierarchy-assets-problems.md#56-hierarchy-assets-and-problems-panels)): a small floating panel, above everything in the viewport, holding the muted label "Name" and a 200 px text field. Its top-left corner sits 8 px right of and below its **anchor point**, then it is moved as little as needed to stay inside the viewport host:
  - with the pointer over the viewport at the F2 press, the pointer;
  - otherwise the object's point on screen, in the displayed pose: the root position for the root, the origin for a body, the shape's center for a shape (circle and box `center`, polygon centroid), the anchor for a joint; the viewport center when that point is outside the viewport or not finite.

  It does not follow the camera or the pointer once open, blocks no navigation, and closes on commit or cancel by the rules of §5.6.
- **Preview environment** (editor only; never exported, except `preview.gravity`):
  - optional ground line, by default at the lowest point of the rest pose;
  - *pin rootBody* option to hang the ragdoll;
  - gravity.
- **Accessibility:** the viewport host is focusable (`tabindex="0"`) and labelled "Viewport". Since it handles keys itself (navigation above), it uses `role="application"`, with `aria-roledescription="viewport"` and `aria-describedby` pointing to a visually hidden summary of its keys: "Space and drag, or middle drag, pans. Wheel, plus and minus zoom. F frames the selection, Home frames everything." Features that add viewport keys extend the summary; the tools add "Escape cancels the current tool operation, or clears the selection. Z switches the transform pivot between the selection center and the last selected object." That way screen readers pass single-key hotkeys ([§5.9](05-9-hotkeys.md#59-hotkeys-defaults)) to the editor instead of using them for browse-mode navigation. The role applies only to the viewport host, never to the page or a panel. Selecting in the viewport needs a pointer; the Hierarchy offers the same selection by keyboard ([§5.6](05-6-hierarchy-assets-problems.md#56-hierarchy-assets-and-problems-panels)).
