# 12. Open items / to verify

- **Smallest shapes:** the resize handles stop at 0.01 m (box sides, circle radius; [§5.5](05-5-tools.md#55-tools)), and the Box and Circle tools accept any size above 0 with grid snap off. Verify in the pinned liquidfun.js build how a box of 0.01 m sides (Box2D's polygon skin is 0.01 m, `b2_linearSlop` 0.005 m) and a circle of 0.01 m radius behave in collisions and joints, and whether validation ([§6.4](06-file-format.md#64-validation)) should warn below a threshold.
- **Degenerate polygons:** [§6.4](06-file-format.md#64-validation) rejects polygons with two vertices closer than 0.0025 m (half of `b2_linearSlop`, Box2D's weld distance) or collinear consecutive vertices. Verify in the pinned liquidfun.js build what `b2PolygonShape.Set` does with welded, collinear or nearly flat input (assertions are likely compiled out), and tighten or relax the thresholds to match.

- **Resolved – spawn binding calls:** verified in the pinned build by `tests/liquidfun-spawn.test.js` (boolean getters such as `IsLocked` return `1`/`0`, not `true`/`false`): the calls [§7.2](07-ragimporter.md#72-spawn-and-flipx) uses that the #5 spike did not cover: `Box2D._malloc`, `Box2D._free` and `Box2D.HEAPF32` with `b2PolygonShape.Set(wrapPointer(ptr, b2Vec2), count)` (vertex read-back and no heap growth over repeated spawns), the four-argument `SetAsBox(hx, hy, center, angle)`, `b2CircleShape.set_m_p` / `set_m_radius`, `b2FixtureDef` setters, `b2RevoluteJointDef` `set_enableLimit` / `set_lowerAngle` / `set_upperAngle` / `set_enableMotor` / `set_collideConnected`, `b2World.IsLocked`, `GetBodyCount`, `GetJointCount`, and `b2Body.SetLinearDamping` / `SetAngularDamping`.
- **Contact filter edge cases:** verify in the pinned build that the joint `collideConnected` check runs before the JS filter (as in upstream Box2D), and what an exception thrown by `ShouldCollide` (for example by a host predicate) does to `world.Step` and the module ([§7.5](07-ragimporter.md#75-contact-filter-and-host-integration)).
- **Resolved – contact filter integration:** RagImporter installs one `JSContactFilter` per world that reimplements Box2D's default rule, adds the self-collision rule through a pointer-keyed registry and ANDs an optional host predicate; `releaseWorld` uninstalls and destroys it; the `groupIndex` fallback remains for builds without `JSContactFilter` and hosts that opt out ([§2.5](02-object-model.md#25-collision-rules), [§7.5](07-ragimporter.md#75-contact-filter-and-host-integration)). Verified in Node by `tests/liquidfun-contact-filter.test.js`.
- **Resolved – liquidfun.js API surface:** the pinned build exposes `b2RevoluteJointDef.Initialize`, the `b2RevoluteJoint` motor, limit, angle and speed methods (after `Box2D.castObject`), fixture filter data, `b2MouseJointDef` / `b2MouseJoint.SetTarget`, `world.DestroyBody` / `DestroyJoint`, `getPointer`, `compare` and `Box2D.destroy` (§3, §7.2). Heap behavior and allocation rules are in §7.4. Verified in Node by `tests/liquidfun-api.test.js`. Side effect: in Node the build installs a `process.on('uncaughtException')` handler.
- **Resolved – liquidfun.js build:** pinned to the doebi/liquidfun.js v1.1.0 Emscripten (asm.js) build. It exposes a single global `Box2D` namespace with WebIDL accessors (§7). Checksum and provenance in `docs/development.md`.
- **Resolved – PIXI.js version:** pinned to 8.21.0. `Assets`, `sortableChildren`/`zIndex` and `eventMode` + `pointermove` were verified in the browser (`docs/development.md`).
- **Motor stability (partly resolved):** a 1.6 kg creature (bodies 6–284 g, chains of up to 6 links) at 60 Hz with 8/3 iterations gains energy on ground contact with `maxTorque` 80 N·m on every joint (kinetic energy up to 52 J) and settles at 1 N·m; a humanoid of 0.3–1.4 kg bodies is stable at 80 (`tests/liquidfun-motor-stability.test.js`). Torque now scales with mass through the suggestion of [§3.1](03-physics-drive-model.md#31-suggested-motor-torque). Still to verify in the pinned build: that the creature with every joint at its suggestion settles (peak kinetic energy below 0.05 J) and the humanoid still holds its pose and follows clips, then tune α (100 rad/s²) and the `W_MOTOR_OVERPOWERED` factor (1000) if not; behavior with high `gain` and with procedural overrides that jump far from the clip angle.
- **Balance controller ([§3.2](03-physics-drive-model.md#32-balance-controller-rootbody-orientation)):** verify in the pinned build that `b2Body.ApplyTorque(torque, wake)`, `GetAngle` (continuous, not wrapped) and `GetAngularVelocity` exist with these signatures and allocate nothing. Measure headless (60 Hz, 8/3, gravity −10) that a humanoid and the 1.6 kg creature with the suggested balance torque hold `rootBody` within a few degrees of a constant target and of a keyed 90° tilt, without chatter, at root `strength` 1, 0.3 and fading to 0; the `lerp(I_root, I_tot, S)` inertia is a heuristic that may need tuning, and if the explicit torque proves unstable with loose joints, evaluate a `b2MotorJoint` (zero `maxForce`) to a fixtureless static body, if the binding exposes it. Also check that the Pose tool's per-step `SetTransform` of `rootBody` in Animate mode ([§5.5](05-5-tools.md#55-tools)) does not make the jointed children jitter.
- **Sprite file access in the browser:** directory pickers are not available in every browser. The multi-file fallback must be usable with 20+ images.
- **Performance:** measure the cost of `update()` + `sync()` with many instances (target: 20 humanoids at 60 Hz without noticeable cost).
- **Resolved – mode switching with a folded Timeline:** the header mode switch ([§5.3](05-3-header.md#53-header)) shows and changes the mode whatever the Timeline's state.

- **Pose from a physics pose:** verify in the pinned build that a Pose test started from scrub-simulation transforms (bodies teleported with `SetTransform` from the rest-pose spawn, joints keeping their rest reference angles) gives `GetJointAngle()` values that match the displayed relative angles within 0.1°, also through reversed joints and after joint drift (stretched anchors) in the scrub simulation, and that the posed preview and the FK pose of the written keys differ only by `rootBody`'s position ([§5.1](05-1-modes.md#51-editor-modes), [§5.5](05-5-tools.md#55-tools)).
- **Viewport input and line rendering:** verify in the browser, with the pinned PIXI.js 8.21.0, that strokes stay 1 CSS px and crisp under a scaled world container and at `devicePixelRatio` > 1 (`pixelLine` strokes or a redraw with width `1 / scale`), and that redrawing the grid on every camera change is cheap; that the wheel factors of [§5.4](05-4-viewport.md#54-viewport) feel alike for mouse wheels and trackpad pinch (`ctrlKey`) in Chromium, Firefox and Safari, and that a non-passive `wheel` listener prevents page zoom; and that a middle-button press does not start autoscroll or, on Linux, paste. Also that with `sharedTicker: true` the application's render runs on `Ticker.shared` after the viewport's redraw callbacks (added at normal priority, render at low), so scene and grid changes appear in the same frame, and that destroying the application leaves `Ticker.shared` running.
- **Viewport tool input:** verify in Chromium, Firefox and Safari that pointer capture keeps delivering moves and the release outside the canvas, and that `lostpointercapture` and window blur reliably end a drag; that a middle press during a left drag keeps the capture and pans with a physical mouse (with events injected through the Chrome DevTools Protocol, Chromium released the capture and the drag was aborted, possibly because the injected `buttons` mask dropped the left button; if a real browser does the same, specify a fix, such as re-capturing while the primary button is still down, in place of the [§5.4](05-4-viewport.md#54-viewport) known limitation); that releasing ALT over the focused viewport does not move focus to the browser menu bar (Firefox on Windows) once its `keyup` is prevented; that Ctrl + click on macOS arrives as a left press with `ctrlKey` and not only as a context menu; and that ALT + drag is not taken by the window manager on common Linux desktops. Also check that redrawing the screen-space gizmo layer and the name labels (PIXI `Text` or `BitmapText`) once per frame stays cheap with 50 bodies and joints ([§5.4](05-4-viewport.md#54-viewport), [§5.5](05-5-tools.md#55-tools)).
- **Alt + letter hotkeys:** verify in Chromium, Firefox and Safari, on Windows, macOS and Linux, that Alt+G (Split, [§5.9](05-9-hotkeys.md#59-hotkeys-defaults)) reaches the page as a `keydown` with `altKey` (and on macOS `code` `KeyG` with an Option character as `key`), that preventing it triggers no browser action and does not move focus to the Firefox menu bar, and that no common window manager takes it.
- **Pose test tuning:** verify in the pinned build that `b2MouseJointDef` takes `set_bodyA`, `set_bodyB`, `set_target`, `set_maxForce`, `set_frequencyHz` and `set_dampingRatio` (Box2D 2.3 names), and that `filter.maskBits = 0` on every shape stops all contacts.
