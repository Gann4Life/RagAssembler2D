# 12. Open items / to verify

- **Smallest shapes:** the resize handles stop at 0.01 m (box sides, circle radius; [§5.5](05-5-tools.md#55-tools)), and the Box and Circle tools accept any size above 0 with grid snap off. Verify in the pinned liquidfun.js build how a box of 0.01 m sides (Box2D's polygon skin is 0.01 m, `b2_linearSlop` 0.005 m) and a circle of 0.01 m radius behave in collisions and joints, and whether validation ([§6.4](06-file-format.md#64-validation)) should warn below a threshold.
- **Degenerate polygons:** [§6.4](06-file-format.md#64-validation) rejects polygons with two vertices closer than 0.0025 m (half of `b2_linearSlop`, Box2D's weld distance) or collinear consecutive vertices. Verify in the pinned liquidfun.js build what `b2PolygonShape.Set` does with welded, collinear or nearly flat input (assertions are likely compiled out), and tighten or relax the thresholds to match.

- **Resolved – spawn binding calls:** verified in the pinned build by `tests/liquidfun-spawn.test.js` (boolean getters such as `IsLocked` return `1`/`0`, not `true`/`false`): the calls [§7.2](07-ragimporter.md#72-spawn-and-flipx) uses that the #5 spike did not cover: `Box2D._malloc`, `Box2D._free` and `Box2D.HEAPF32` with `b2PolygonShape.Set(wrapPointer(ptr, b2Vec2), count)` (vertex read-back and no heap growth over repeated spawns), the four-argument `SetAsBox(hx, hy, center, angle)`, `b2CircleShape.set_m_p` / `set_m_radius`, `b2FixtureDef` setters, `b2RevoluteJointDef` `set_enableLimit` / `set_lowerAngle` / `set_upperAngle` / `set_enableMotor` / `set_collideConnected`, `b2World.IsLocked`, `GetBodyCount`, `GetJointCount`, and `b2Body.SetLinearDamping` / `SetAngularDamping`.
- **Contact filter edge cases:** verify in the pinned build that the joint `collideConnected` check runs before the JS filter (as in upstream Box2D), and what an exception thrown by `ShouldCollide` (for example by a host predicate) does to `world.Step` and the module ([§7.5](07-ragimporter.md#75-contact-filter-and-host-integration)).
- **Resolved – contact filter integration:** RagImporter installs one `JSContactFilter` per world that reimplements Box2D's default rule, adds the self-collision rule through a pointer-keyed registry and ANDs an optional host predicate; `releaseWorld` uninstalls and destroys it; the `groupIndex` fallback remains for builds without `JSContactFilter` and hosts that opt out ([§2.5](02-object-model.md#25-collision-rules), [§7.5](07-ragimporter.md#75-contact-filter-and-host-integration)). Verified in Node by `tests/liquidfun-contact-filter.test.js`.
- **Resolved – liquidfun.js API surface:** the pinned build exposes `b2RevoluteJointDef.Initialize`, the `b2RevoluteJoint` motor, limit, angle and speed methods (after `Box2D.castObject`), fixture filter data, `b2MouseJointDef` / `b2MouseJoint.SetTarget`, `world.DestroyBody` / `DestroyJoint`, `getPointer`, `compare` and `Box2D.destroy` (§3, §7.2). Heap behavior and allocation rules are in §7.4. Verified in Node by `tests/liquidfun-api.test.js`. Side effect: in Node the build installs a `process.on('uncaughtException')` handler.
- **Resolved – liquidfun.js build:** pinned to the doebi/liquidfun.js v1.1.0 Emscripten (asm.js) build. It exposes a single global `Box2D` namespace with WebIDL accessors (§7). Checksum and provenance in `docs/development.md`.
- **Resolved – PIXI.js version:** pinned to 8.21.0. `Assets`, `sortableChildren`/`zIndex` and `eventMode` + `pointermove` were verified in the browser (`docs/development.md`).
- **Motor stability (resolved by substeps):** the energy gain and trembling of light rigs (a 1.6 kg creature on 11–23 g foot pieces) were the solver not converging through light bodies at one 1/60 s step. With the 8 substeps of [§3.1](03-physics-drive-model.md#31-suggested-motor-torque) the creature, a humanoid, a snake, the demo sample and a light-hub biped hold their poses within 1° with the worst-case suggested torques and settle with a peak kinetic energy below 0.05 J (`tests/liquidfun-motor-stability.test.js`, `tests/liquidfun-hold.test.js`, `tests/liquidfun-firmness.test.js`). Still to verify: behavior with high `gain`, with fast keyed segments under the feed-forward of #209, and with procedural overrides that jump far from the clip angle, and the cost of 8 substeps with many instances (see Performance).
- **Balance controller ([§3.2](03-physics-drive-model.md#32-balance-controller-rootbody-orientation)):** verify in the pinned build that `b2Body.ApplyTorque(torque, wake)`, `GetAngle` (continuous, not wrapped) and `GetAngularVelocity` exist with these signatures and allocate nothing. Measure headless (60 Hz, 8/3, gravity −10) that a humanoid and the 1.6 kg creature with the suggested balance torque hold `rootBody` within a few degrees of a constant target and of a keyed 90° tilt, without chatter, at root `strength` 1, 0.3 and fading to 0; the rigid wrench over the whole connected set (inertia of `R` about its common center, [§3.2](03-physics-drive-model.md#32-balance-controller-rootbody-orientation)) assumes the ragdoll turns as one piece, which loose joints (low `strength`) break: if it proves unstable there, evaluate a `b2MotorJoint` (zero `maxForce`) to a fixtureless static body, if the binding exposes it.
- **Keyed velocity feed-forward (#209, [§3](03-physics-drive-model.md#3-physics-drive-model-springs-via-motors)):** measure the acceptance metrics M1–M5 headless. Verify in the pinned build that `b2_maxRotation` is 0.5π rad per step and that a motor speed far above the reachable one (a keyed 50+ rad/s) gives no energy spike. Verify that α = 1000 rad/s² (§3.1) keeps the 1.6 kg creature and the light-hub biped free of energy gain with the 8 substeps (α ≈ 1200 failed on that creature at one step without substeps, 2026-10-23); if it does not, lower α to the largest value that passes (not below 400, which the punch needs) and record the measurement. If M1 fails with α = 1000 because the motors saturate, the lever is the user-set torque below, not a larger α. Measured (2026-10-25): M1–M4 pass on the test rig and on a user robot rig (fast segments of 16–18 rad/s arrive within 2° at 1.0 × speed), but with 8 velocity iterations the robot's legs rang at about 12 Hz (solver not converging through its 20 g thighs, carried by warm starting; independent of α, torque, gains and contacts), so `STEP_SETTINGS.velocityIterations` is 80 (M6, [§3.1](03-physics-drive-model.md#31-suggested-motor-torque)). Still to measure: the velocity-solver cost of 80 iterations in host scenes with many awake contacts, and whether fewer substeps suffice now. Follow-ups out of scope: authored follow-through (how far a joint may pass a fast key; today only torque, contacts and limits bound it) and a rate for fades (weight changes add no rate today).
- **User-set motor torque (next after #209; the owner's per-joint torque lever, with the joint inspector page):** today every `motor.maxTorque` and `balance.maxTorque` is automatic ([§5.6](05-6-hierarchy-assets-problems.md#56-hierarchy-assets-and-problems-panels)), so a value set by hand in a file is replaced on Load. When the inspector lets the user type a torque, the editor must know which values are user-set. Options: (a) an `auto` flag per joint motor and on `balance` (a format change: version 3, migrated with `auto: true` everywhere, matching today's behavior), the inspector field showing "auto" or the value with a "Reset to automatic" button, and a Rig › Reset motor torque to automatic item (selected joints, or all); (b) no format change, a value counted automatic while it equals the suggestion of the document before the edit, which breaks whenever the §3 formulas change (every saved value then reads as user-set). (a) is recommended. `W_MOTOR_OVERPOWERED` and `W_MOTOR_UNDERPOWERED` ([§6.4](06-file-format.md#64-validation)) then also warn on user-set values in the editor. Also to measure: the cost of running both suggestion functions after every rig command on a 50-body rig (expected well under 1 ms).
