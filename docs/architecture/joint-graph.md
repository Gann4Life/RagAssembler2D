# Joint graph

`js/model/queries.js` (core, #16 creates it with the first three functions below; #17 adds `parentJoints` and `bodySubtree`; later issues add `referencesTo`, `spriteUsers` and extend `jointTree` as they need). Pure reads of the model, no events, no caching: callers call them when they need a fresh answer.

- **Joint edges.** A joint is an edge when its `bodyA` and `bodyB` props are file ids that resolve (`model.uidOf`) to two **different** `body` nodes; other joints (a `null`, unknown or non-body reference, or `bodyA == bodyB`) are ignored by all three functions. Edges are undirected: a joint connects its bodies whichever is bodyA ([`DESIGN.md` §2.4](../design/02-object-model.md#24-joints)).
- **`jointTree(model) → JointTree`**, `JointTree { root: number | null, depth: Map<bodyUid, number>, parent: Map<bodyUid, jointUid>, loops: Set<jointUid> }`. `root` is the uid of the body `rootBody` names, or `null` when it is `null`, unknown or not a body; then every map and set is empty. Otherwise a breadth-first search from `root`: `depth(root) = 0`; bodies are dequeued in discovery order, and for each one its edges are visited in `joints` order; an edge to a body not yet discovered is a **tree joint**: that body gets `depth + 1`, `parent` = the joint, and is enqueued. `depth` therefore is the smallest number of joints between a body and `rootBody`, and the spanning tree is deterministic. `loops` holds every edge whose two bodies are both reachable and which is not a tree joint ([`DESIGN.md` §2.4](../design/02-object-model.md#24-joints) loop joints; a second joint between the same pair is one). Bodies missing from `depth` are unlinked; edges among unlinked bodies are in neither `parent` nor `loops`.
- **`jointBetween(model, bodyUid1, bodyUid2) → jointUid | null`**: the first edge in `joints` order whose two bodies are those uids, in either order; `null` when there is none or the uids are equal.
- **`orderJointBodies(model, bodyUid1, bodyUid2) → { bodyA, bodyB }`**: the [`DESIGN.md` §2.4](../design/02-object-model.md#24-joints) creation rule. The body with the smaller `jointTree(model).depth` is `bodyA`, a body without a depth counting as `Infinity`; on equal depths (both unlinked included) the body earlier in `model.children(ROOT_UID, 'bodies')` is `bodyA`. The result does not depend on argument order. Throws `Error` when a uid is not a body or the uids are equal.
- **`parentJoints(model) → Map<bodyUid, jointUid>`** (#17): the parent joint of every body that has one ([`DESIGN.md` §2.4](../design/02-object-model.md#24-joints), Parent joint and subtree). With `tree = jointTree(model)`: a body in `tree.depth` takes `tree.parent.get(body)` (`rootBody` has no entry; loop joints never appear); a body not in `tree.depth` (unlinked, or every body when `tree.root` is `null`) takes the first edge in `joints` order whose `bodyB` resolves to it (an edge, so `bodyA` resolves to another body; such an edge never touches a linked body). Bodies without a parent joint have no entry. Map iteration follows `bodies` order. Computed in one pass over the edges after one `jointTree` call.
- **`bodySubtree(model, bodyUid, parents = parentJoints(model)) → Set<bodyUid>`** (#17): `bodyUid` first, then, in `bodies` order, every body whose chain of parent bodies reaches `bodyUid`. A body's parent body is the other body of its parent joint; a chain stops at a body without a parent joint or at a body already seen on that chain, so cycles among unlinked bodies terminate (in a directed cycle every member's subtree is the whole cycle). On linked bodies it equals the descendants in the `jointTree` spanning tree; loop joints are never followed. `parents` lets callers reuse one `parentJoints` result across several calls. Throws `Error` when `bodyUid` is not a body.
- Imports: `js/model/model.js` (`ROOT_UID`).
